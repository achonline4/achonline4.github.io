How it works

FAQ

Is it hard to set up?

No. Install the app on both devices and pair them with a 6-digit code. That's it.

Does anyone else keep a copy of my files?

No. All user data is E2E encrypted, so no one can read / alter them in transit.

What happens when my home device is off?

Your files can't be reached until it's back on. Keep it plugged in and switched on.

Which devices can I use?

Laptops, Desktops, Mini PCs, Mobile phone, any computer that can install an app and stay connected to the internet.

Is there a size limit?

Only the free storage on your home device. If your connection drops, a big upload or download carries on where it stopped.

How it works

Three steps, no accounts.

Start the app, add files, and share the address it shows.

1

Add files to Hosting

Press Add or drop files on the window. Tick Require permission on anything private.

2

Open the address

On any device on the same Wi-Fi, open the address from the banner, like 192.168.1.11:8080.

3

Share both ways

People download what you host and send files back. Anything that needs your OK waits for Allow.

Features

Small app, thoughtful details.

Everything a household, classroom or office needs to move files around a room.

Ask before download

Mark any file "Require permission". The request appears in the window with who is asking, and nothing moves until you allow it.

Approve uploads

With Require approval on, every upload waits for your OK before a single byte is sent. Turn it off for trusted rooms.

List or thumbnails

See files as a list or as tiles with real image previews. Browsers follow the view you pick for Hosting.

Always the latest copy

Edit a hosted file and the next download gets the new version. Each file shows when it was last updated.

Rename for sharing

Give a file a friendlier name for browsers. The extension stays, and the file on your disk keeps its real name.

Received to Hosting

One tap on the arrow moves a received file over to Hosting, ready for everyone else to download.

Select and tidy

Checkboxes and Select all in both sections. Unshare in bulk, or clear out received files after a confirmation.

Resumable, with progress

Live progress bars on both sides, two uploads at a time, and downloads that resume if the Wi-Fi blinks.

Looks at home in light or dark.

The window follows your desktop's theme. Thumbnail tiles show real previews of your pictures and colourful type tiles for everything else.

  • A one-click copy button next to the address
  • Desktop notifications when someone asks
  • Files that vanish from disk leave the list on their own

A page that just works, on anything.

Visitors get a clean page with search, previews, a drop zone and live progress. It works offline on the local network and follows each device's light or dark mode.

  • "Ask to download" waits, then saves by itself
  • Drag and drop uploads with speed and progress
  • Built for phones first

Private by design.

Your files never leave the room. LAN Server talks only to devices on your own network, and only you decide what goes out or comes in.

  • Local network onlyConnections from outside private and link-local ranges are turned away at the door.
  • Unguessable grantsAn allowed download is a random 128-bit ticket, tied to the device that asked, valid for 30 minutes.
  • No sneak previewsFiles that need permission never show their picture to browsers before you allow them.
  • No accounts, no cloudNo sign-up, no tracking, no internet needed: just your computer and your Wi-Fi.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work on Windows or macOS?

The app that hosts the files runs on Linux. Everyone who connects can use any system with a browser: Windows, macOS, Android, iPhone, iPad, ChromeOS or Linux.

Do the other devices need to install anything?

No. They open the address shown in the window (for example http://192.168.1.11:8080) in their browser, and that's it.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything happens over your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet. The page has no outside fonts, scripts or trackers, so it works even when the internet is down.

Where do uploaded files go?

Into a LanServer folder inside your Downloads folder. The folder button in the Received section opens it.

Is it safe on a shared or public Wi-Fi?

Anyone on the same network can open the page, so keep private files on "Require permission" and leave upload approval on. Files never leave your network, and only devices on it can connect.

What happens if I change a file while it is hosted?

The next download gets the new version, and the file's "updated" time and size change on their own. If you delete or move a hosted file, it leaves the list automatically.
